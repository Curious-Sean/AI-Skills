# Examples
Five worked notes, the artefact itself rather than a document about it: each starts at the frontmatter, as a saved note does. Read the one whose map type is closest to the chosen variant, not all five.

| File | Map type | Material | What it shows |
|---|---|---|---|
| [`example-nested.md`](example-nested.md) | `flowchart`, a path to a decision | Schallleitung gegen Schallempfindung | Three levels of nesting, four offloads as flowchart, table and measured curve (`xychart-beta`), and `Die zwei Formen im Vergleich` as a `#` heading beside the Karte |
| [`example-states.md`](example-states.md) | `stateDiagram-v2`, one ear through its states | Paukenerguss und Paukendrainage | The coupling on a simple state, because a `classDef` reaches no composite state, an offload of another type than its map, and a table one level deeper |
| [`example-classes.md`](example-classes.md) | `classDiagram`, entities with shared fields | Hörgerät, Cochlea-Implantat, BAHA | Three devices filling the same three fields, so the comparison is the diagram itself, and inheritance beside a station of the hearing path |
| [`example-ishikawa.md`](example-ishikawa.md) | `ishikawa-beta`, several influences onto one finding | Rezidivierende Otitis media beim Kleinkind | Three levels, where two ribs carry groups of causes, a map that takes no colour, so the shared word alone couples the two offloads and their headings stay black, and a fishbone with no legend, because nothing in it is invisible |
| [`example-quadrant.md`](example-quadrant.md) | `quadrantChart`, a position on two axes | Schwindelformen | Two questions from the history as the axes, the coupling colour on a single point, and an offload that adds what the position cannot show |

What all five hold to: every line carries a label or a legend line, a legend is written only where a style or a series colour is invisible and sits under the picture, one line each, lines cross frame borders where the relation does, and the arrowhead stays for what really follows.
Another material picks another type: [`../reference/type-inventory.md`](../reference/type-inventory.md). The rules behind all of this: [`../reference/drawing-conventions.md`](../reference/drawing-conventions.md).
